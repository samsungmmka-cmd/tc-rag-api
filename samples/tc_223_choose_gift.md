# Galaxy Choose-Your-Gift Promotion 2026 - Terms and Conditions

## Promotion period

The promotion is valid for qualifying purchases made between 1 September 2026 and 30 November 2026 (inclusive).

## Qualifying devices

Qualifying devices are the Samsung Galaxy A55, Galaxy S25 and Galaxy S25 Ultra only.

## Participating retailers

Purchases must be made at MTN Stores, Vodacom Shops or Takealot. Purchases from other retailers do not qualify.

## Eligibility

Open to South African residents with a valid South African ID number.

## Claim window

Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date.

## Limits

One claim per IMEI/serial number and one claim per ID number per campaign.

## Proof of purchase

The invoice must show the invoice number, store name, purchase date and device IMEI or serial number.

## Gifts

When submitting the claim, the customer may choose one of the following gifts: Samsung Galaxy Buds3, R750 Takealot voucher, Samsung 25W Travel Adapter.

## Gift values

| Gift | Retail value |
|---|---|
| Samsung Galaxy Buds3 | R2 999 |
| R750 Takealot voucher | R750 |
| Samsung 25W Travel Adapter | R399 |
